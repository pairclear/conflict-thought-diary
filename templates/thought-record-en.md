## 4. Any thinking traps?

_These are common patterns that make a fight feel worse than the facts say. Tick the ones you recognise in your thought. It is fine if none fit._

- [ ] **Mind reading**: "He's quiet, so he's angry with me"
- [ ] **Fortune telling**: "We'll fight again and it will end in a breakup"
- [ ] **Catastrophising**: "That's it, the relationship is ruined"
- [ ] **Always and never**: "You never listen to me"
- [ ] **Labelling**: "Selfish", "toxic", "narcissist"
- [ ] **Taking it personally**: "They're in a bad mood, so I must have done something wrong"
- [ ] **All on one person**: "It's all their fault" or "It's all me"
- [ ] **Shoulds**: "If he loved me, he should guess what I need"
- [ ] **Emotional reasoning**: "I feel abandoned, so I am being abandoned"
- [ ] **Mental filter**: Out of the whole evening you remember only one sharp remark

## 6. What else could be true?

_Look for other explanations. You don't have to believe them; just let them sit next to the first thought._

- What might my partner have been feeling or thinking at that moment?
- What would I tell a friend who told me this story?
- How will this look in a week? In a year?
- Is there a part I would do differently next time?
